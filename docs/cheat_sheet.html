System Gestures & Control Commands Firmware State Machine Automations
TAP SHIFT
Backspace / Delete: Tap the Shift key alone (press and quickly release without composing any dots) to delete the previous character.
HOLD 4+5+6
Language Toggle: Hold dots 4 + 5 + 6 together for 500 ms to switch instantly between English and Bangla modes.
SPACE KEY
Space Insertion: Press dedicated space thumb switch. Built-in edge-detection prevents unintended runaway repetition.
SHIFT + VOWEL
Bangla Kar / Matra: In Bangla mode, holding Shift while striking any vowel chord produces its dependent vowel sign (e.g. া, ি, ী, ু).
